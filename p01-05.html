Product Assembly Manual: Flower

This model is based on the reproductive structure of a typical camellia flower. Assemble all components in the order below.

Step 1. Install the protective base layer

  • Component Sepal
  • Purpose Protects the developing flower bud and supports the outer base of the flower.
  • Method Arrange the sepals in a circular formation at the base and secure them in place.

Step 2. Attach the corolla

  • Component Petal
  • Purpose Surrounds the reproductive organs and helps attract pollinators.
  • Method Fix the petals around the centre to form the corolla.

Step 3. Assemble the stamens

  • Component Stamen
  • Purpose Produces and releases pollen.
  • Method Position the stamens around the central pistil.

Step 4. Install the pistil

  • Component Pistil
  • Purpose Receives pollen and contains the ovules that develop into seeds after fertilisation.
  • Method Fix the pistil upright at the centre. Verify that all components are securely connected.
